Welcome to Green Valley

A Peaceful Escape Designed Around Nature

Green Valley brings together private open spaces, natural surroundings and thoughtfully planned lifestyle amenities. It is a place where your family can slow down, reconnect and enjoy weekends away from the rush of everyday city life.

Owning a private weekend retreat means more than just property.it is a commitment to a pace of life that puts family, nature and well-being first. At Green Valley, every plot is an opportunity to build something that is entirely your own: a farmhouse, a weekend home, a garden that breathes.

Situated at Khanderavpura, close to the tranquil natural setting near Thol, Green Valley enjoys the quiet character of Gujarat's countryside yet remains comfortably connected to Kadi, Mehsana and the broader Ahmedabad region for everyday convenience.
